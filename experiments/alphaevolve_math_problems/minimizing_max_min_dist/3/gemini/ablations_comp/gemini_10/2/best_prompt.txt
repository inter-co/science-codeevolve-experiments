SETTING:

Your task is to implement a robust **global optimization algorithm**, preferably a **population-based metaheuristic** (e.g., Genetic Algorithm, Differential Evolution, Particle Swarm Optimization), to find an optimal arrangement of exactly 14 points in 3D space. The algorithm should iteratively refine point positions (or populations of solutions) to maximize the ratio of minimum distance to maximum distance between all point pairs, specifically within a bounded search space.

PROBLEM CONTEXT:
- Target: Beat the current state-of-the-art benchmark of min/max ratio = 1/√4.165849767 ≈ 0.4898
- Constraint: Points **must be strictly confined within a unit cube [0,1]³**. This implies that point coordinates (x, y, z) must always satisfy 0 ≤ x, y, z ≤ 1. For population-based metaheuristics, consider direct boundary handling or penalty methods to enforce these bounds effectively. While solutions on a unit sphere are an alternative geometric approach, the primary focus for this task is the unit cube.
- Mathematical formulation: For points Pi = (xi, yi, zi), i = 1,...,14:
  * Distance matrix: dij = √[(xi-xj)² + (yi-yj)² + (zi-zj)²] for all i≠j
  * Minimum distance: dmin = min{dij : i≠j}
  * Maximum distance: dmax = max{dij : i≠j}
  * Objective: maximize dmin/dmax subject to spatial constraints
