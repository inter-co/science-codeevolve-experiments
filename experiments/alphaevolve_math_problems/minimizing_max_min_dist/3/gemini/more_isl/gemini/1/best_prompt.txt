Your task is to implement an iterative optimization process within a constructor function. Prioritize the exploration of distinct algorithmic pathways:
1.  **Population-Based Metaheuristics**: Implement a Genetic Algorithm (GA) or Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) as the primary optimization engine, leveraging their ability to explore diverse regions of the search space simultaneously.

  * Maximum distance: dmax = max{dij : i≠j}
  * Objective: maximize dmin/dmax subject to spatial constraints
