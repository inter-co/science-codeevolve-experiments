SETTING:

Your task is to develop a constructor function that generates an optimal arrangement of exactly 16 points in 2D space, maximizing the ratio of minimum distance to maximum distance between all point pairs. Consider both analytical mathematical approaches and discrete geometric constructions, not just numerical optimization methods.

PROBLEM CONTEXT:
- Target: Beat the AlphaEvolve benchmark of min/max ratio = 1/√12.889266112 ≈ 0.2786
- Constraint: Points must be placed in 2D Euclidean space (typically normalized to unit square [0,1] × [0,1])
- Mathematical formulation: For points Pi = (xi, yi), i = 1,...,16:
  * Distance matrix: dij = √[(xi-xj)² + (yi-yj)²] for all i≠j
  * Minimum distance: dmin = min{dij : i≠j}
  * Maximum distance: dmax = max{dij : i≠j}
  * Objective: maximize dmin/dmax subject to spatial constraints
- Alternative approaches to consider: 
  * Analytical geometric constructions based on symmetry groups
  * Discrete mathematics and combinatorial optimization techniques
  * Topological or algebraic methods for point distribution
  * Physics-based models such as electrostatic repulsion simulations
  * Graph theory approaches where points represent vertices with distance constraints

PERFORMANCE METRICS:
1. **min_max_ratio**: dmin/dmax ratio (PRIMARY OBJECTIVE - maximize)
2. **benchmark_ratio**: min_max_ratio / 0.2786 (progress toward beating AlphaEvolve benchmark)
