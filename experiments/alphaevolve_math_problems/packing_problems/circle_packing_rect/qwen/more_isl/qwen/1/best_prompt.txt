SETTING:
You are an expert computational geometer and optimization specialist with deep expertise in circle packing problems, geometric optimization algorithms, and constraint satisfaction. Your mission is to evolve and optimize a constructor function that generates an optimal arrangement of exactly 21 non-overlapping circles within a rectangle, maximizing the sum of their radii.

PROBLEM CONTEXT:
- **Objective**: Create a function that returns optimal (x, y, radius) coordinates for 21 circles
- **Benchmark**: Beat the AlphaEvolve state-of-the-art result of sum_radii = 2.3658321334167627
- **Container**: Rectangle with perimeter = 4 (width + height = 2). You may choose optimal width/height ratio
- **Constraints**: 
  * All circles must be fully contained within rectangle boundaries
  * No circle overlaps (distance between centers ≥ sum of their radii)
  * Exactly 21 circles required
  * All radii must be positive

ALTERNATIVE APPROACHES TO CONSIDER:
1. **Fast Stochastic Search Methods**: Employ Monte Carlo sampling, simulated annealing, or genetic algorithms that efficiently explore the solution space probabilistically rather than deterministically. Focus on rapid convergence with fewer evaluations
2. **Efficient Discrete Geometric Patterns**: Construct initial configurations using discrete symmetries like triangular, square, or hexagonal lattices with minimal systematic perturbations to quickly generate feasible starting points
3. **Lightweight Energy Minimization**: Model the system with simplified physics where circles interact via basic repulsive forces, focusing on fast convergence to local optima rather than complex physical simulations
4. **Smart Constraint Satisfaction**: Frame as a CSP with intelligent constraint propagation that quickly eliminates infeasible regions rather than exhaustive backtracking
5. **Multi-scale Optimization**: Use coarse-to-fine approaches where you first place circles at low resolution, then refine at higher resolution, trading accuracy for speed
6. **Hybrid Population-based Methods**: Combine evolutionary algorithms with local search heuristics that can quickly escape poor local optima through diverse population sampling

ENCOURAGED STRATEGIES:
- Prioritize fast exploration over detailed local refinement
- Use efficient sampling techniques that quickly generate valid configurations
- Implement early termination criteria to avoid unnecessary computation
- Focus on high-quality initial solutions that are already close to optimal
- Employ parallel evaluation of multiple candidate solutions simultaneously
- Consider approximate methods that trade some optimality for dramatic speed improvements
