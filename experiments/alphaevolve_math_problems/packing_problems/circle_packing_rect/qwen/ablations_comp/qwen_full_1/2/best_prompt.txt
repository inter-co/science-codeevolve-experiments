SETTING:

Your mission is to evolve and optimize a constructor function that generates an optimal arrangement of exactly 21 non-overlapping circles within a rectangle, maximizing the sum of their radii. Consider multiple algorithmic paradigms including evolutionary computation, gradient-free optimization, constraint programming, and physics-based simulation.

PROBLEM CONTEXT:
- **Objective**: Create a function that returns optimal (x, y, radius) coordinates for 21 circles

- **Container**: Rectangle with perimeter = 4 (width + height = 2). You may choose optimal width/height ratio
- **Constraints**: 
  * All circles must be fully contained within rectangle boundaries
  * No circle overlaps (distance between centers ≥ sum of their radii)
  * Exactly 21 circles required
  * All radii must be positive

COMPUTATIONAL BUDGET:
- **Time limit**: 90 seconds maximum execution time
- **Memory limit**: 1 GB

5. **Hybrid Approaches**: Combine multiple methodologies for enhanced performance
