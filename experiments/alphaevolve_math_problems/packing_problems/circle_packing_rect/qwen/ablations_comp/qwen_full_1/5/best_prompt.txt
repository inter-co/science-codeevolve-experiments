SETTING:

Consider multiple algorithmic paradigms including evolutionary computation, gradient-free optimization, geometric construction heuristics, and constraint satisfaction methods.

- **Objective**: Create a function that returns optimal (x, y, radius) coordinates for 21 circles

- **Constraints**:

  * No circle overlaps (distance between centers ≥ sum of their radii)
  * Exactly 21 circles required
  * All radii must be positive

COMPUTATIONAL BUDGET:
- **Time limit**: 90 seconds maximum execution time
- **Memory limit**: 1 GB

**Implementation flexibility**: Feel free to choose one primary approach or combine multiple methodologies. The key is to demonstrate creative problem-solving through novel algorithmic combinations rather than simple brute force.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:
- **Determinism**: Use fixed random seeds if employing stochastic methods for reproducibility
- **Error handling**: Graceful handling of optimization failures or infeasible configurations
