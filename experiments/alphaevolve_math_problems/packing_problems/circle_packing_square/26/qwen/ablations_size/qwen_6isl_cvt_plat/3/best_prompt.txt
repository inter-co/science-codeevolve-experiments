Your task is to implement a sophisticated algorithm that generates an optimal arrangement of exactly 26 non-overlapping circles within a unit square [0,1] × [0,1], maximizing the sum of their radii.

This is a classic and highly challenging circle packing problem that requires advanced optimization techniques. The goal is to beat the AlphaEvolve benchmark of sum_radii = 2.6358627564136983.

PROBLEM CONTEXT:
- Target: Beat the AlphaEvolve benchmark of sum_radii = 2.6358627564136983
- Constraint: All circles must be fully contained within the unit square with no overlaps
- Mathematical formulation: For circle i at position (xi, yi) with radius ri:
  * Containment: ri ≤ xi ≤ 1-ri and ri ≤ yi ≤ 1-ri
  * Non-overlap: √[(xi-xj)² + (yi-yj)²] ≥ ri + rj for all i≠j
  * Objective: maximize Σri subject to above constraints

This is a well-known NP-hard optimization problem that typically requires:
- Advanced global optimization algorithms (simulated annealing, genetic algorithms, or particle swarm optimization)
- Spatial data structures for efficient collision detection (KDTree, spatial hashing)
- Careful initialization strategies to avoid local optima
- Multi-start approaches to explore the solution space effectively
