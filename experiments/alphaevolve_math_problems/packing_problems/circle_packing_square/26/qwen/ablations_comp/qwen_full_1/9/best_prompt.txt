SETTING:

PROBLEM CONTEXT:
- Target: Beat the AlphaEvolve benchmark of sum_radii = 2.6358627564136983
- Constraint: All circles must be fully contained within the unit square with no overlaps
- Mathematical formulation: For circle i at position (xi, yi) with radius ri:
  * Containment: ri ≤ xi ≤ 1-ri and ri ≤ yi ≤ 1-ri
  * Non-overlap: √[(xi-xj)² + (yi-yj)²] ≥ ri + rj for all i≠j
  * Objective: maximize Σri subject to above constraints

4. **Multi-Start Heuristics**: Combine multiple initialization strategies (random, grid-based, spiral) with local optimization - explores diverse starting configurations
5. **Constraint Programming**: Formulate as a constrained optimization problem using constraint programming libraries like OR-Tools or python-constraint - may handle complex constraints more robustly than mathematical programming
6. **Monte Carlo Methods**: Use random sampling with importance weighting or Markov Chain Monte Carlo to explore the feasible region probabilistically
7. **Gradient-Free Optimization**: Explore derivative-free methods like Nelder-Mead, COBYLA, or direct search algorithms that don't rely on gradient information
8. **Hybrid Metaheuristics**: Combine simulated annealing with local search, or particle swarm optimization with geometric constraints
