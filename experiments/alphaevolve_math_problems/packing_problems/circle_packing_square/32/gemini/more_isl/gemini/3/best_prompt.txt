# PROMPT-BLOCK-START

SETTING:
You are an expert computational geometer, physicist, and optimization specialist, exploring diverse strategies for solving complex circle packing problems.
Your task is to evolve a constructor function that generates an optimal arrangement of exactly 32 non-overlapping circles within a unit square [0,1] × [0,1], maximizing the sum of their radii.

PROBLEM CONTEXT:
- Target: Beat the AlphaEvolve benchmark of sum_radii = 2.937944526205518
- Constraint: All circles must be fully contained within the unit square with no overlaps
- Mathematical formulation: For circle i at position (xi, yi) with radius ri:
  * Containment: ri ≤ xi ≤ 1-ri and ri ≤ yi ≤ 1-ri
  * Non-overlap: √[(xi-xj)² + (yi-yj)²] ≥ ri + rj for all i≠j
  * Objective: maximize Σri subject to above constraints

DIVERSE ALGORITHMIC STRATEGIES:
To foster innovation and explore distinct solution landscapes, consider the following high-level algorithmic paradigms:
*   **Physics-based Simulation**: Model circles as interacting particles (e.g., repulsive forces between circles, attractive forces towards the center, or confinement forces from boundaries) and simulate their movement to find stable, dense packings. Libraries like `pymunk` can be highly effective here.
*   **Constructive Heuristics**: Develop algorithms that build the packing incrementally. This could involve sequential placement (e.g., largest circle first, or placing circles in available "gaps"), or grid-based initializations followed by local adjustments.
*   **Advanced Metaheuristics**: Beyond standard evolutionary algorithms, explore techniques like Simulated Annealing, Particle Swarm Optimization, or hybrid approaches combining local search with global exploration.
*   **Constraint Programming / Satisfiability (CP/SAT)**: Formulate the circle packing conditions as a set of constraints and an objective function, then leverage powerful solvers like `ortools` or `python-constraint`.
