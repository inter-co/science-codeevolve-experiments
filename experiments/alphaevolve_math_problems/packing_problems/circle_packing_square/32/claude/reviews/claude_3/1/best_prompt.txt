SETTING:
You are an expert in physics-based simulation and computational geometry specializing in circle packing through energy minimization.
Your task is to evolve a constructor function that generates an optimal arrangement of exactly 32 non-overlapping circles within a unit square [0,1] × [0,1], maximizing the sum of their radii through physics-inspired dynamics and force equilibrium.

PROBLEM CONTEXT:
- Target: Beat the AlphaEvolve benchmark of sum_radii = 2.937944526205518
- Constraint: All circles must be fully contained within the unit square with no overlaps
- Physical interpretation: Model circles as particles with repulsive forces that push overlapping circles apart while boundary forces keep them contained
- Energy-based formulation:
  * Overlap penalty energy: E_overlap = Σ(i<j) max(0, ri+rj - dij)² where dij is distance between circles i,j
  * Boundary penalty energy: E_boundary = Σi [max(0, ri-xi)² + max(0, xi+ri-1)² + max(0, ri-yi)² + max(0, yi+ri-1)²]
  * Radius reward: R = -Σri (negative because we minimize energy but maximize radii)
  * Total energy: E = E_overlap + E_boundary + λ·R (balance penalties with radius maximization)
- Algorithmic paradigms to consider:
  * Force-directed relaxation: Apply repulsive forces between overlapping circles
  * Simulated annealing with thermal expansion: Start with small radii, gradually increase while allowing positional adjustments
  * Molecular dynamics: Treat circles as soft spheres with collision dynamics
  * Gradient descent on energy landscape with adaptive step sizes
