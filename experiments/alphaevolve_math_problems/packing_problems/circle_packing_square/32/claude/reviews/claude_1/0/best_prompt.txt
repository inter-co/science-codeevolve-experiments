SETTING:
You are an expert in mathematical optimization and convex geometry with deep knowledge of packing theory.
Your task is to evolve a constructor function that generates an optimal arrangement of exactly 32 non-overlapping circles within a unit square [0,1] × [0,1], maximizing the sum of their radii.

CRITICAL INSIGHT: Circle packing is a highly nonconvex global optimization problem. Local refinement methods (gradient descent, physics simulation, greedy expansion) often converge to suboptimal local minima. Consider leveraging:
- **Global optimization frameworks** that provide escape mechanisms from local optima
- **Theoretical packing bounds** from Apollonian gaskets, hexagonal lattice theory, or density bounds
- **Dual formulations** or relaxations that transform the problem into more tractable forms
- **Stochastic global search** with strong diversification mechanisms

PROBLEM CONTEXT:
- Target: Beat the AlphaEvolve benchmark of sum_radii = 2.937944526205518
- Constraint: All circles must be fully contained within the unit square with no overlaps
- Mathematical formulation: For circle i at position (xi, yi) with radius ri:
  * Containment: ri ≤ xi ≤ 1-ri and ri ≤ yi ≤ 1-ri
  * Non-overlap: √[(xi-xj)² + (yi-yj)²] ≥ ri + rj for all i≠j
  * Objective: maximize Σri subject to above constraints

THEORETICAL CONSIDERATIONS:
- **Packing density**: Optimal circle packing in the plane achieves π/(2√3) ≈ 0.9069 density (hexagonal lattice)
- **Square boundary effects**: Edge constraints reduce achievable density; consider boundary-aware strategies
- **Scale invariance**: The problem exhibits multi-scale structure; circles of varying sizes may pack more efficiently than uniform radii
- **Apollonian gasket inspiration**: Recursive space-filling with circles of decreasing size
- **Upper bounds**: Theoretical maximum sum_radii can be estimated via area arguments (total area = π Σri²)

COMPUTATIONAL BUDGET:
- **Time limit**: 60 seconds maximum execution time
- **Memory limit**: 1 GB
