Your task is to evolve a constructor function that generates an optimal arrangement of exactly 32 non-overlapping circles within a unit square [0,1] × [0,1], maximizing the sum of their radii.

ALGORITHMIC PARADIGM:
Consider physics-inspired approaches where circles behave as particles subject to:
- **Repulsive forces** between overlapping circles (proportional to overlap depth)
- **Boundary forces** pushing circles away from square edges when they exceed bounds
- **Expansion pressure** allowing radii to grow when spatial constraints permit
- **Energy minimization** through iterative relaxation until equilibrium

INITIALIZATION STRATEGY:
Rather than starting from empty configurations, consider:
- **Hexagonal lattice seeds**: Initialize positions on a regular hexagonal grid pattern (optimal for infinite plane packing)
- **Stratified random placement**: Divide the square into regions and place circles with guaranteed minimum separation
- **Progressive growth**: Start with small equal-sized circles and incrementally expand radii while maintaining feasibility

- Constraint: All circles must be fully contained within the unit square with no overlaps
- Mathematical formulation: For circle i at position (xi, yi) with radius ri:
  * Containment: ri ≤ xi ≤ 1-ri and ri ≤ yi ≤ 1-ri
  * Non-overlap: √[(xi-xj)² + (yi-yj)²] ≥ ri + rj for all i≠j
  * Objective: maximize Σri subject to above constraints

- **Symmetry exploitation**: The unit square has 4-fold rotational symmetry; symmetric configurations may approach optimality
