Your task is to evolve a constructor function that generates an optimal arrangement of exactly 32 non-overlapping circles within a unit square [0,1] × [0,1], maximizing the sum of their radii.
Consider multiple algorithmic paradigms including but not limited to:
- Mathematical programming formulations (linear/nonlinear optimization)
- Physics-based simulation approaches (repulsive forces, molecular dynamics)
- Constraint satisfaction techniques with advanced constraint propagation
