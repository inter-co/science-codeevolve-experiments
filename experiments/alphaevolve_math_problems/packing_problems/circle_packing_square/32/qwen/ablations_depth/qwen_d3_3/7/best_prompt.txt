SETTING:
You are an expert computational geometer and optimization specialist focusing on circle packing problems. Your task is to develop a sophisticated constructor function that generates an optimal arrangement of exactly 32 non-overlapping circles within a unit square [0,1] × [0,1], maximizing the sum of their radii.

PROBLEM CONTEXT:
- Target: Beat the AlphaEvolve benchmark of sum_radii = 2.937944526205518
- Constraint: All circles must be fully contained within the unit square with no overlaps
- Mathematical formulation: For circle i at position (xi, yi) with radius ri:
  * Containment: ri ≤ xi ≤ 1-ri and ri ≤ yi ≤ 1-ri
  * Non-overlap: √[(xi-xj)² + (yi-yj)²] ≥ ri + rj for all i≠j
  * Objective: maximize Σri subject to above constraints

ALGORITHMIC APPROACHES TO CONSIDER:
1. **Stochastic Optimization**: Explore simulated annealing, tabu search, or other metaheuristics that naturally handle complex constraint landscapes
2. **Machine Learning Approaches**: Consider neural networks or reinforcement learning to learn optimal circle placement patterns
3. **Mathematical Programming**: Formulate as a nonlinear programming problem using advanced solvers like IPOPT or BARON
4. **Geometric Construction**: Develop constructive algorithms based on tessellation principles or packing theory
5. **Multi-start Strategies**: Implement systematic exploration with multiple random initializations and local searches
6. **Decomposition Methods**: Break into subproblems (e.g., cluster circles first, then optimize within clusters)
