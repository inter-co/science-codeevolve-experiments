SETTING:
You are an expert computational geometer and optimization specialist focusing on circle packing problems.
Your task is to implement a constructor function that generates an optimal arrangement of exactly 32 non-overlapping circles within a unit square [0,1] × [0,1], maximizing the sum of their radii.

PROBLEM CONTEXT:
- Target: Beat the AlphaEvolve benchmark of sum_radii = 2.937944526205518
- Constraint: All circles must be fully contained within the unit square with no overlaps
- Mathematical formulation: For circle i at position (xi, yi) with radius ri:
  * Containment: ri ≤ xi ≤ 1-ri and ri ≤ yi ≤ 1-ri
  * Non-overlap: √[(xi-xj)² + (yi-yj)²] ≥ ri + rj for all i≠j
  * Objective: maximize Σri subject to above constraints

1. **Evolutionary Algorithms**: Implement genetic algorithms or differential evolution to iteratively improve circle arrangements
2. **Physics-based Simulation**: Use particle physics or force-directed placement with repulsive forces

4. **Geometric Construction**: Explore tessellation-based approaches or Voronoi diagrams for initial placements
5. **Hybrid Methods**: Combine multiple techniques such as starting with a good heuristic then refining with local optimization
