Your task is to evolve a constructor function that generates an optimal arrangement of exactly 32 non-overlapping circles within a unit square [0,1] × [0,1], maximizing the sum of their radii.

PROBLEM CONTEXT:
- Target: Beat the AlphaEvolve benchmark of sum_radii = 2.937944526205518
- Constraint: All circles must be fully contained within the unit square with no overlaps
- Mathematical formulation: For circle i at position (xi, yi) with radius ri:
  * Containment: ri ≤ xi ≤ 1-ri and ri ≤ yi ≤ 1-ri
  * Non-overlap: √[(xi-xj)² + (yi-yj)²] ≥ ri + rj for all i≠j
  * Objective: maximize Σri subject to above constraints
- **Alternative algorithmic pathways**:

  * Constraint programming: encode the placement constraints in a CP‑SAT solver (`ortools.sat`) and search for feasible solutions that maximize the objective.  
  * Evolutionary / swarm methods: use custom crossover that preserves non‑overlap, or particle swarm optimization with position‑based constraints.  
  * Voronoi‑guided placement: iteratively refine positions by moving circles towards the centroids of their Voronoi cells while maintaining separation.  
  * Divide‑and‑conquer: partition the square into sub‑regions, solve smaller packing problems, then merge while resolving boundary conflicts.  
  * Machine‑learning heuristics: train a surrogate model to predict good initial radii and positions, then refine with local search.  
  * Hybrid pipelines: combine any of the above, e.g., start with a CP‑SAT solution, then perform gradient refinement.
