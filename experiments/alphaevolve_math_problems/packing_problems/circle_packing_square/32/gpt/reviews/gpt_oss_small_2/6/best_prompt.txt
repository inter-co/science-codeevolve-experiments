You are encouraged to explore a diverse set of algorithmic paradigms—including evolutionary algorithms, constraint programming, physics-based simulation, gradient-based optimization, and geometric decomposition (e.g., Voronoi or circle inversion)—to discover novel packing configurations that may surpass the AlphaEvolve benchmark.

- Employ Voronoi diagrams to partition the square into cells, assign one circle per cell, and adjust radii to fit within cells.
