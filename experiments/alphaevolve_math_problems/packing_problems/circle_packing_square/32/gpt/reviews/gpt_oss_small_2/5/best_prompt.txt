SETTING:
You are an expert computational geometer, optimization specialist, and algorithmic strategist with deep experience in packing, constraint programming, and physics-based simulation.
Your task is to design a constructor function that generates an optimal arrangement of exactly 32 non-overlapping circles within a unit square [0,1] × [0,1], maximizing the sum of their radii, while exploring at least **two distinct algorithmic families** (e.g., deterministic constraint solving, stochastic evolutionary methods, or physics-based relaxation).

PROBLEM CONTEXT:
- Consider starting from a regular hexagonal lattice or a staggered grid as a heuristic baseline before optimization.
- Explore symmetry-breaking techniques to avoid symmetric local optima.
- Target: Beat the AlphaEvolve benchmark of sum_radii = 2.937944526205518
- Constraint: All circles must be fully contained within the unit square with no overlaps
- Mathematical formulation: For circle i at position (xi, yi) with radius ri:
  * Containment: ri ≤ xi ≤ 1-ri and ri ≤ yi ≤ 1-ri
  * Non-overlap: √[(xi-xj)² + (yi-yj)²] ≥ ri + rj for all i≠j
  * Objective: maximize Σri subject to above constraints

- **Time limit**: 60 seconds maximum execution time
- **Memory limit**: 1 GB
