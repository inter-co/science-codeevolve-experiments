SETTING:
You are a multidisciplinary optimization engineer with expertise in computational geometry, constraint programming, physics simulation, and evolutionary algorithms. Your goal is to design a flexible constructor that can generate an optimal arrangement of exactly 32 non‑overlapping circles inside the unit square [0,1]×[0,1], maximizing the total sum of radii. Encourage exploration of at least three distinct algorithmic families (e.g., physics‑based packing, constraint‑programming, and evolutionary search) and document the rationale for each approach.

PROBLEM CONTEXT:
- Target: Surpass the AlphaEvolve benchmark of sum_radii = 2.937944526205518.
- Constraints: Every circle must lie entirely within the unit square and no two circles may overlap.
- Formulation: For circle i at (xi, yi) with radius ri:
  * Containment: ri ≤ xi ≤ 1‑ri and ri ≤ yi ≤ 1‑ri.
  * Non‑overlap: √[(xi‑xj)² + (yi‑yj)²] ≥ ri + rj for all i≠j.
  * Objective: maximize Σri subject to the above constraints.
- Additional Considerations: Treat the radii as continuous variables; positions are continuous; consider Lagrangian relaxation or penalty methods for soft constraints. Explore both global and local search strategies.

COMPUTATIONAL BUDGET:
- **Time limit**: 60 seconds maximum execution time
- **Memory limit**: 1 GB

COMPUTATIONAL RESOURCES:
**Core packages**: numpy, scipy, sympy, pandas, networkx, jax, torch, numba, scikit‑learn

**Optimization**: `deap` (evolutionary algorithms), `platypus` (multi‑objective), `pulp` or `cvxpy` (linear / convex programming), `ortools` (constraint programming)

**Geometric computing**: `shapely` (geometric operations), `rtree` (spatial indexing), `scipy.spatial` (KDTree, Voronoi), `pygraphviz` (graph layout for visualization)

**Physics simulation**: `pymunk` (2D physics), `pybullet` (physics engine), `box2d` (rigid body dynamics)

**Performance**: `cython`, `numba`, `joblib` (parallelization), `jax` (just‑in‑time compilation)

PERFORMANCE METRICS:
1. **sum_radii**: Total sum of all 32 circle radii (PRIMARY OBJECTIVE – maximize).
2. **benchmark_ratio**: sum_radii / 2.937944526205518 (progress toward beating benchmark).
3. **eval_time**: Execution time in seconds (keep reasonable; prioritize accuracy over speed).
4. **feasibility_rate**: Fraction of candidate solutions that satisfy all constraints during search.
5. **iteration_count**: Number of iterations or generations executed by the chosen algorithm.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:
- **Determinism**: Use fixed random seeds for any stochastic components to ensure reproducibility.
- **Error handling**: Gracefully handle optimization failures or infeasible configurations; provide fallback strategies.
- **Memory efficiency**: Avoid large dense distance matrices; use spatial indexing or approximate nearest neighbors.
- **Scalability**: Design modules so that the same code can be adapted to different numbers of circles with minimal changes.
- **Modularity**: Separate concerns into distinct functions: feasibility checker, objective evaluator, solver interface, and result formatter.
- **Logging**: Emit progress logs (e.g., best objective value per iteration) for debugging and analysis.
