SETTING:
You are an expert computational geometer and optimization specialist focusing on circle packing problems.
Your task is to design a constructor function that generates an optimal arrangement of exactly 32 non-overlapping circles within a unit square [0,1] × [0,1], maximizing the sum of their radii.
You should explore a diverse set of algorithmic paradigms: constraint programming, physics simulation, gradient-based local search, and evolutionary strategies. Emphasize novel packing patterns beyond standard lattice or random placement, such as hexagonal tiling, iterative insertion, or Voronoi-based refinement.

PROBLEM CONTEXT:
- Target: Beat the AlphaEvolve benchmark of sum_radii = 2.937944526205518
- Constraint: All circles must be fully contained within the unit square with no overlaps
- Mathematical formulation: For circle i at position (xi, yi) with radius ri:
  * Containment: ri ≤ xi ≤ 1-ri and ri ≤ yi ≤ 1-ri
  * Non-overlap: √[(xi-xj)² + (yi-yj)²] ≥ ri + rj for all i≠j
  * Objective: maximize Σri subject to above constraints

COMPUTATIONAL BUDGET:
- **Time limit**: 60 seconds maximum execution time
- **Memory limit**: 1 GB

COMPUTATIONAL RESOURCES:
**Core packages**: numpy, scipy, sympy, pandas, networkx, jax, torch, numba, scikit-learn

**Additional useful packages**:
- **Optimization**: `deap` (evolutionary algorithms), `platypus` (multi-objective optimization), `pulp` (linear programming), `cvxpy` (convex optimization)
- **Geometric computing**: `shapely` (geometric operations), `rtree` (spatial indexing), `scipy.spatial` (KDTree, Voronoi), `pyclipper` (polygon clipping)
- **Constraint programming**: `python-constraint`, `ortools` (Google OR-Tools), `z3-solver` (SMT solver)
- **Physics simulation**: `pymunk` (2D physics), `pybullet` (physics engine), `pydiffusion` (diffusion-based placement)
- **Performance**: `cython`, `numba`, `joblib` (parallelization), `jax` (just-in-time compilation)

PERFORMANCE METRICS:
1. **sum_radii**: Total sum of all 32 circle radii (PRIMARY OBJECTIVE - maximize)
2. **benchmark_ratio**: sum_radii / 2.937944526205518 (progress toward beating benchmark)
3. **packing_density**: Ratio of total circle area to unit square area (secondary objective to encourage compactness)
4. **feasibility_rate**: Fraction of generated configurations that satisfy all constraints (ensures robustness)
5. **eval_time**: Execution time in seconds (keep reasonable, prefer accuracy over speed)

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:
- **Determinism**: Use fixed random seeds if employing stochastic methods for reproducibility
- **Error handling**: Graceful handling of optimization failures or infeasible configurations, with fallback to simpler heuristics
- **Memory efficiency**: Avoid excessive memory allocation for distance matrix computations; use spatial indexing or lazy evaluation
- **Scalability**: Design with potential extension to different circle counts in mind; modular representation of circle parameters
- **Algorithmic diversity**: Encourage the use of at least two distinct optimization paradigms (e.g., constraint programming + physics simulation) within a single solution to compare performance
