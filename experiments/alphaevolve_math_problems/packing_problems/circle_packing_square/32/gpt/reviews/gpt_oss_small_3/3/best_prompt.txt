ALTERNATIVE APPROACHES:
- **Constraint Programming**: Formulate the packing as a finite set of linear/non‑linear constraints and solve with a CP‑solver (e.g., OR‑Tools, python‑constraint). This can provide exact feasibility checks and exploit symmetry reductions.
- **Physics‑Based Relaxation**: Treat circles as repulsive particles and let a physics engine (e.g., pymunk, pybullet) relax them into a low‑energy configuration that respects containment. This often yields high‑quality initial guesses for subsequent local optimization.
- **Evolutionary / Multi‑objective Search**: Use DEAP or Platypus to evolve populations of circle configurations, possibly balancing sum of radii against other metrics (e.g., minimal pairwise distance). Multi‑objective Pareto fronts can reveal trade‑offs that a single‑objective search might miss.
- **Gradient‑Based Local Refinement**: Once a feasible layout is found, apply a continuous optimization routine (e.g., SciPy’s `minimize` with constraints) to fine‑tune radii and positions, exploiting smoothness of the objective and constraints.
- **Simulated Annealing / Randomized Hill‑Climbing**: Perform stochastic perturbations of circle positions/radii while accepting moves that improve the objective or occasionally worse ones to escape local optima.
- **Voronoi / Delaunay‑Based Heuristics**: Use the Voronoi diagram of candidate centers to enforce spacing constraints and guide radius assignments, leveraging geometric duality for efficient feasibility checks.
- **Hybrid Strategies**: Combine two or more of the above (e.g., physics relaxation followed by gradient refinement) to leverage complementary strengths.
