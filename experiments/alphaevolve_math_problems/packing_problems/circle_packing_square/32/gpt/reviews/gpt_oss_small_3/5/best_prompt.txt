SETTING:
You are an expert computational geometer and optimization specialist focusing on circle packing problems.
Your goal is to design a flexible, extensible algorithm that can generate high-quality arrangements of exactly 32 non-overlapping circles within a unit square [0,1] × [0,1], aiming to maximize the sum of their radii. You should explore multiple algorithmic paradigms (e.g., evolutionary, physics-based, constraint programming, geometric heuristics) and select the most promising strategy or combine them for improved performance.

PROBLEM CONTEXT:
- Target: Beat the AlphaEvolve benchmark of sum_radii = 2.937944526205518
- Constraint: All circles must be fully contained within the unit square with no overlaps
- Mathematical formulation: For circle i at position (xi, yi) with radius ri:
  * Containment: ri ≤ xi ≤ 1-ri and ri ≤ yi ≤ 1-ri
  * Non-overlap: √[(xi-xj)² + (yi-yj)²] ≥ ri + rj for all i≠j
  * Objective: maximize Σri subject to above constraints

COMPUTATIONAL BUDGET:
- **Time limit**: 60 seconds maximum execution time (allowing for multiple algorithmic trials)
- **Memory limit**: 1 GB
- **Parallelism**: You may spawn multiple processes or threads to run independent algorithmic experiments in parallel, aggregating the best result.

ALTERNATIVE APPROACHES:
1. **Evolutionary Algorithms**: Use `deap` or `platypus` to evolve circle positions and radii with custom fitness.
2. **Physics-Based Simulation**: Treat circles as soft bodies and let them settle under repulsive forces; use `pymunk` or `pybullet`.
3. **Constraint Programming**: Encode non-overlap and containment as constraints in OR-Tools or Z3 and solve with a CP-SAT solver.
4. **Geometric Heuristics**: Generate a lattice or hexagonal packing, then locally adjust radii using Voronoi cells or Delaunay triangulation.
5. **Hybrid Methods**: Combine a coarse geometric layout with a fine-grained evolutionary refinement.
