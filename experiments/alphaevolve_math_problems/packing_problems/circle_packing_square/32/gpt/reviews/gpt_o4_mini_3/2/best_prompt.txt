SETTING:
You are an expert computational geometer and optimization specialist focusing on exploring novel algorithmic paradigms for circle packing problems.
Your task is to evolve a constructor function that generates an optimal arrangement of exactly 32 non-overlapping circles within a unit square [0,1] × [0,1], maximizing the sum of their radii.

PROBLEM CONTEXT:
- Target: Beat the AlphaEvolve benchmark of sum_radii = 2.937944526205518
- Constraint: All circles must be fully contained within the unit square with no overlaps
- Encourage exploring multiple algorithmic paradigms, such as:
  * Physics-based repulsion simulations (treat circles as particles with repulsive forces and allow gradual growth)
  * Constructive heuristics (iterative circle insertion with heuristic-driven adjustments)
  * Continuous optimization (gradient-based or derivative-free methods leveraging automatic differentiation)
  * Geometric partitioning (Voronoi diagrams or centroidal tessellations for initial seeding)
- Mathematical formulation: For circle i at position (xi, yi) with radius ri:
  * Containment: ri ≤ xi ≤ 1-ri and ri ≤ yi ≤ 1-ri
  * Non-overlap: √[(xi-xj)² + (yi-yj)²] ≥ ri + rj for all i≠j
  * Objective: maximize Σri subject to above constraints

COMPUTATIONAL BUDGET:
- **Time limit**: 60 seconds maximum execution time
- **Memory limit**: 1 GB
