SETTING:
You are an interdisciplinary problem‑solver with expertise in computational geometry, numerical optimization, and physics‑based simulation.  
Your mission is to design a *constructor* that places exactly 32 non‑overlapping circles inside the unit square [0,1] × [0,1] while **maximizing the total sum of radii**.  
Encourage the assistant to explore a **diverse set of algorithmic paradigms**—from analytical geometric constructions and convex relaxation to stochastic meta‑heuristics and physics engines—rather than a single canonical approach.  
The solution should be modular, reproducible, and ready to be extended to other circle counts or objective variants (e.g., weighted radii, packing density).

PROBLEM CONTEXT:
- Target: Beat the AlphaEvolve benchmark of sum_radii = 2.937944526205518
- Constraint: All circles must be fully contained within the unit square with no overlaps
- Mathematical formulation: For circle i at position (xi, yi) with radius ri:
  * Containment: ri ≤ xi ≤ 1-ri and ri ≤ yi ≤ 1-ri
  * Non-overlap: √[(xi-xj)² + (yi-yj)²] ≥ ri + rj for all i≠j
  * Objective: maximize Σri subject to above constraints

**Strategic Guidance**  
1. **Geometric Decomposition** – Use Voronoi diagrams or circle inversion to generate candidate configurations that respect the boundary constraints.  
2. **Convex Relaxation** – Formulate a semidefinite program that bounds the optimal sum and use the solution as a warm‑start for a local search.  
3. **Physics‑Based Simulation** – Treat circles as soft bodies in a 2D physics engine (e.g., pymunk) and let them settle into a low‑energy state; then refine with a penalty‑based optimizer.  
4. **Evolutionary Algorithms** – Deploy DEAP or platypus with custom mutation operators that preserve feasibility (e.g., radius scaling coupled with positional jitter).  
5. **Hybrid Approaches** – Combine a global search (e.g., simulated annealing) with a local gradient‑based refinement (jax or autograd) to escape local optima.  
These pathways should be explored independently; the assistant is encouraged to prototype at least two distinct strategies and compare their performance on the benchmark.
