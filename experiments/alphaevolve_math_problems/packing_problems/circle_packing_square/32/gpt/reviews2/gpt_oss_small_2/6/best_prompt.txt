SETTING:
You are an expert computational geometer and optimization specialist focusing on circle packing problems.
Your task is to evolve a constructor function that generates an optimal arrangement of exactly 32 non-overlapping circles within a unit square [0,1] × [0,1], maximizing the sum of their radii.
You must avoid trivial, static grid placements; instead, the solution should compute positions dynamically and justify each algorithmic choice.
Additionally, you are encouraged to explore a diverse set of algorithmic strategies beyond conventional evolutionary approaches, including but not limited to constraint programming, physics-based simulation, gradient-based continuous optimization, analytical geometry, and hybrid metaheuristics. The solution should be modular enough to allow swapping of optimization backends and should aim to discover novel packing configurations that may surpass existing benchmarks.

PROBLEM CONTEXT:
- Target: Beat the AlphaEvolve benchmark of sum_radii = 2.937944526205518
- Constraint: All circles must be fully contained within the unit square with no overlaps
- Mathematical formulation: For circle i at position (xi, yi) with radius ri:
  * Containment: ri ≤ xi ≤ 1-ri and ri ≤ yi ≤ 1-ri
  * Non-overlap: √[(xi-xj)² + (yi-yj)²] ≥ ri + rj for all i≠j
  * Objective: maximize Σri subject to above constraints
- The algorithm must dynamically compute positions; reliance on a static grid layout is disallowed.
- All containment checks must be performed before final output; any circle violating the unit square boundaries must be corrected or discarded.

COMPUTATIONAL BUDGET:
- **Time limit**: 60 seconds maximum execution time, but solutions may leverage parallelism or just-in-time compilation to stay within limits.
- **Memory limit**: 1 GB; algorithms should monitor memory usage and avoid large dense matrices when possible.
