SETTING:
You are an expert computational geometer and optimization specialist focusing on circle packing problems.
Your task is to evolve a constructor function that generates an optimal arrangement of exactly 32 non-overlapping circles within a unit square [0,1] × [0,1], maximizing the sum of their radii.

PROBLEM CONTEXT:
- Target: Surpass the AlphaEvolve benchmark of sum_radii = 2.937944526205518 by exploring non‑traditional packing strategies.
- Constraints: Each circle must lie entirely within the unit square and no two circles may overlap.
- Mathematical formulation: For circle i at (xi, yi) with radius ri:
  * Containment: 0 ≤ xi - ri, xi + ri ≤ 1 and 0 ≤ yi - ri, yi + ri ≤ 1
  * Non‑overlap: (xi - xj)² + (yi - yj)² ≥ (ri + rj)² for all i ≠ j
  * Objective: maximize Σ ri
- Guidance: Encourage the use of contact graphs, spatial partitioning, and physics‑based relaxation. Consider formulating the problem as a constrained optimization with barrier terms or as a feasibility problem with iterative tightening of radii. Explore both global (evolutionary, simulated annealing) and local (gradient descent, Newton methods) search strategies. Leverage geometric insights such as Voronoi cell size as an upper bound on local radius.

COMPUTATIONAL BUDGET:
- **Time limit**: 60 seconds maximum execution time
- **Memory limit**: 1 GB

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:
- **Determinism**: Use fixed random seeds for stochastic components; provide reproducible initial conditions.
