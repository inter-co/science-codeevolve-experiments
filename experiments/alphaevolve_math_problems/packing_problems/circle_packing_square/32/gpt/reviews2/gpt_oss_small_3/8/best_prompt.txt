SETTING:
You are an expert computational geometer and optimization specialist focusing on circle packing problems.
Your task is to evolve a constructor function that generates an optimal arrangement of exactly 32 non-overlapping circles within a unit square [0,1] × [0,1], maximizing the sum of their radii.

PROBLEM CONTEXT:

ALTERNATIVE APPROACHES:
- **Geometric Heuristics**: Use Voronoi diagrams or Delaunay triangulation to identify the largest empty circles iteratively, then place a circle at the center of each empty region.
- **Physics‑Based Simulation**: Treat circles as soft bodies with repulsive forces and use a physics engine (pymunk, pybullet) to let them settle into a dense packing.
- **Constraint Programming**: Encode containment and non‑overlap constraints in CP‑SAT (ortools) or python‑constraint and solve with a solver that can handle nonlinear geometric constraints.
- **Evolutionary Algorithms**: Use DEAP or Platypus to evolve populations of circle configurations, applying crossover and mutation on positions and radii, and selecting for the largest sum of radii.
- **Simulated Annealing / Basin‑Hopping**: Randomly perturb positions/radii and accept moves according to a temperature schedule, allowing occasional uphill moves to escape local optima.
- **Gradient‑Based Continuous Optimization**: Formulate the problem as a differentiable objective (sum radii) with constraints and use JAX or PyTorch autograd to perform projected gradient descent or Adam‑style updates.
- **Mixed‑Integer Linear/Quadratic Programming**: Linearize the non‑overlap constraints using big‑M or piecewise linear approximations and solve with OR‑Tools CP‑SAT or a MILP solver.
- **Hierarchical / Multi‑Phase Packing**: First place a coarse grid of circles with fixed radii, then iteratively inflate radii while resolving overlaps, or reverse the process by shrinking large circles to free space for smaller ones.
- **Local Search / Hill Climbing**: Start from a feasible configuration and iteratively adjust the circle with the smallest radius or the most violated constraint to improve the sum.
- **Randomized Incremental Construction**: Add circles one by one at random positions, then locally adjust to avoid overlaps, repeating until no improvement is observed or a time limit is reached.
- Target: Beat the AlphaEvolve benchmark of sum_radii = 2.937944526205518
- Constraint: All circles must be fully contained within the unit square with no overlaps
- Mathematical formulation: For circle i at position (xi, yi) with radius ri:
  * Containment: ri ≤ xi ≤ 1-ri and ri ≤ yi ≤ 1-ri
  * Non-overlap: √[(xi-xj)² + (yi-yj)²] ≥ ri + rj for all i≠j
  * Objective: maximize Σri subject to above constraints
