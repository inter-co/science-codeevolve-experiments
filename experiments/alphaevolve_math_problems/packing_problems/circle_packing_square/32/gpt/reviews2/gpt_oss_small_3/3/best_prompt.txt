Your task is to design a constructor function that produces an arrangement of exactly 32 non‑overlapping circles inside the unit square [0,1] × [0,1] while maximizing the total sum of radii. Encourage the exploration of diverse algorithmic families (e.g., evolutionary, constraint programming, physics simulation, gradient‑based, greedy, or hybrid approaches) rather than a single canonical method.

PROBLEM CONTEXT:
- Goal: Surpass the AlphaEvolve benchmark of sum_radii = 2.937944526205518.
- Constraints: Circles must lie entirely inside the unit square and must not overlap.
- Formulation: For circle i at (xi, yi) with radius ri:

  * Non‑overlap: distance((xi, yi), (xj, yj)) ≥ ri + rj for all i ≠ j
  * Objective: maximize Σ ri.
- Encourage exploring non‑linear, discrete, and continuous formulations, including but not limited to mixed‑integer linear programming, non‑linear programming with penalty terms, and geometric heuristics.
